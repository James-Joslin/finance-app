# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and respectful environment for everyone participating in the Finova community, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact directly.
- Give and accept technical feedback professionally.
- Respect privacy and never pressure anyone to share personal or financial information.
- Focus disagreements on ideas and evidence rather than individuals.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately misleading others.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing credentials, financial records, database dumps, or other sensitive material.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the Finova project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to project maintainer James Joslin using the contact information available on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/James-Joslin). If no private channel is available, open a minimal issue requesting private contact without describing the incident publicly.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behaviour is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
